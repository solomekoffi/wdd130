Togo

Togo is a country in West Africa, bordered by Ghana to the west, Benin to the east, and Burkina Faso to the north. It has a narrow coastline along the Gulf of Guinea to the south. The country is known for its diverse landscapes, including savannas in the north and lush hills in the south. Lomé, the capital and largest city, serves as the country's main port and center of commerce.
